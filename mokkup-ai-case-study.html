AI-Powered Dashboard Design Recommendations for Mokkup.ai

Helping users create effective dashboards with AI-powered guidance instead of starting from a blank canvas.

Role
Product Designer
Product
Mokkup.ai — 'Ideate'
01

Introduction

A dashboard wireframing platform used by product designers, business analysts, and product teams.

Mokkup.ai is a dashboard wireframing platform used by product designers, business analysts, and product teams to design dashboards quickly.

As dashboard complexity grew, users struggled to choose the right charts, KPIs, and layouts, leading to slower design decisions and increased effort.

02

User & Business Context

Who this feature serves, and what success looks like for the business.

PRIMARY USERS
Product Designers, Business Analysts & Data Teams
Product Managers and data teams building dashboards from raw business data.
USER GOAL

Quickly build meaningful dashboards without needing deep data visualization expertise.

BUSINESS GOAL

Help users create high-quality dashboards faster, reducing the effort required to move from data to insights.

BUSINESS CHALLENGE Many users struggle to choose the right charts, KPIs, and layouts, leading to slower dashboard creation and lower feature adoption.
03

Problem

Users understood what business data they wanted to present but struggled with how to present it effectively.

KEY CHALLENGES
  • Choosing the right chart type
  • Identifying relevant KPIs
  • Structuring dashboard layouts
  • Starting from a blank canvas
RESULT Dashboard creation became slow, repetitive, and heavily dependent on trial and error.
04

Current Workflow

Blank Dashboard
↓
Browse Widgets
↓
Choose Charts
↓
Arrange Layout
↓
Modify & Retry
↓
Publish Dashboard
PAIN POINTS
  • Too many design decisions
  • Frequent trial and error
  • Time-consuming workflow
  • Low confidence in dashboard design
05

Design Strategy

Each friction point in the old workflow was reframed as an AI-assisted decision.

ProblemDesign Strategy
Users don't know where to start.Begin with a business goal or dataset upload.
Too many visualization options create confusion.Recommend the most relevant charts and KPIs.
Dashboard setup is time-consuming.Generate an initial dashboard layout automatically.
Users may not trust AI recommendations.Explain suggestions and allow full customization before publishing.
06

Proposed Workflow

The AI-assisted flow replaces manual trial and error with guided, explainable suggestions.

Business Goal or Upload Data Sheet
↓
AI Understands Context
↓
Recommends Charts
↓
Suggests KPIs
↓
Arrange Layout
↓
User Reviews & Customizes
↓
Publish Dashboard
OUTCOME
  • Fewer decisions
  • Faster workflow
  • Higher confidence
