CASE STUDY / 02
Healthcare SaaS — Appointment Operations

Designing a Unified Appointment Management System for Front Desk Teams

Simplifying appointment operations for providers, hospitals, and patients through a centralized, Kanban-based workflow.

Role
UX / Product Designer
Product
Global Healthcare Platform
01

Introduction

A healthcare management system designed to streamline appointment operations for healthcare providers.

The Global Healthcare Platform (GHP) is a healthcare management system designed to streamline appointment operations for healthcare providers.

This project focuses on the Front Desk Staff, helping them efficiently manage patient appointments through a centralized Kanban-based workflow while keeping patients informed with automated notifications throughout the appointment journey.

02

User & Business Context

Understanding who this platform serves, and why it matters to the business.

PRIMARY USERS
Front Desk Staff / Receptionist
Responsible for managing daily appointments, coordinating with providers, and ensuring a smooth patient journey.
BUSINESS GOAL

Improve operational efficiency by simplifying appointment management and reducing manual coordination.

USER GOAL

Manage appointments quickly, track every patient's status, and communicate updates without switching between multiple tools.

03

Problem

Front desk staff managed appointments using fragmented workflows, making it difficult to organize daily schedules and keep patients informed.

KEY CHALLENGES
  • No clear overview of appointment progress
  • Manual status updates and patient communication
  • Difficult to prioritize appointments
  • Frequent follow-ups through phone calls
RESULT Administrative workload increased, appointment tracking became inefficient, and patients lacked visibility into their appointment status.
04

Current Workflow

Appointment Booked
↓
Front Desk Reviews Schedule
↓

↓

↓

PAIN POINTS
  • Multiple manual follow-ups
  • No centralized appointment tracking
  • Difficult to prioritize appointments
  • Poor visibility into appointment progress
05

Design Decision

Each pain point was mapped directly to a specific design response.

ProblemDesign Decision
Difficult to track appointment progressIntroduce a Kanban-based Appointment Board organized by appointment stages.
Manual patient communicationTrigger Automatic Patient Notifications whenever appointment status changes.
Appointment information spread across different viewsCreate a Centralized Appointment Dashboard with all essential information in one place.
Time spent searching for appointmentsAdd Smart Search & Filters to quickly locate appointments by patient, provider, or status.

OUTCOME
  • Faster appointment management
  • Clear visual tracking of every patient
  • Reduced manual communication
  • Better coordination between staff, providers, and patients
07

Visual Design

Selected screens from the Appointment Management module.

08

Expected Impact

  • Reduced administrative workload
  • Better visibility into appointment status
  • More efficient daily operations
VALIDATION — 3 PROVIDERS

The prototype was walked through with 3 local healthcare providers before development, to test whether the Kanban-based workflow actually matched how front desk staff think about a day's appointments.

What worked
  • Status board made it easy to see the whole day at a glance
  • Automatic notifications meant fewer manual follow-up calls
  • Search & filters made specific appointments faster to find
Requested improvements
  • Wanted to bulk-update several appointments at once
  • Asked for color-coding by appointment type
  • Needed a way to flag urgent or overdue appointments
TAKEAWAY

Centralizing status and communication removes the manual coordination that was slowing the front desk down.
